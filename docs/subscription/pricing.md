# Pricing

Flat-rate per active staff seat. No tiers, no per-client fees, no calls.

![Pricing page](/img/screens/01-pricing.png)

## Locked-in price

- **₹599 / staff / month** (ex-GST)
- **₹5,990 / staff / year** — annual plan, **2 months free** baked in (≈ 17 % off, effective ₹499 / staff / month)
- **Minimum 1 seat** — solo CAs are first-class
- **Talk-to-sales above 25 seats** — Enterprise conversation, dedicated onboarding

## Concessions sales can offer (off-list, capped at 30 % blended)

- **Founding firm** — 30 % off year 1, first 100 firms, locked in for 24 months
- **ICAI member** — 15 % off first annual term, verified by membership number
- **10+ seats** — 5 % volume; 25+ seats → Enterprise conversation
- **Non-profit / education firm** — 50 %, case-by-case

## What's included

The whole product. There is no feature gating between plans — one tier covers compliance calendar, time tracking, GST invoicing, client portal, communications, document management, every module.

## How we arrived at ₹599

Research-grounded against the Indian CA-practice-management market:

- **Legacy desktop + AMC** (KDK Spectrum, SAG Genius, CompuTax, Saral TaxOffice) — ~₹400-600 per seat per month equivalent. That's the budget anchor in a CA partner's head.
- **Modern Indian SaaS** — ₹100-830 per seat per month (QwikCA, Suvit, Zoho Practice). The cheap ones are loss-leading on per-seat to push unlimited tiers.
- **International tools** — Karbon, Canopy, TaxDome at ₹4,000-8,000 / seat / month. CAs feel the UX is worth it, but USD pricing is unaffordable.

₹599 sits at the ₹499/₹999 psychological band, ~5× QwikCA (signals depth, not a race-to-bottom clone), ~1/8 of Karbon (modern alternative without the USD price). A 10-staff firm pays ~₹6k/mo — small enough to expense without partner sign-off, large enough to fund the build.
